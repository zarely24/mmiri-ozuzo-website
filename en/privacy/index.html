Privacy & cookies

This page explains what happens to your data when you visit the Mmiri Ozuzo website and when you book through ebarber. It is written to be read, not to discourage.

1. Data controller

The controller for this website is the barbershop Mmiri Ozuzo (trading as “Mmiri Ozuzo”), Kolokotroni 10, Athens 105 61, Greece, tel. +30 210 331 1323.

No data protection officer (DPO) has been appointed. For any privacy matter, contact us at the details above.

2. What the website does — and does not do

The website is informational. It has no user accounts, contact forms, newsletter, comments or payments. It uses no cookies, local storage, analytics, advertising pixels, session recording or third-party fonts: every file loads from our own server. The only exception is the map, which loads only when you ask for it (section 4).

3. Server logs

When you open a page, the server hosting the website (our hosting provider) automatically receives your IP address, the time, the page requested, your browser type and the page you came from. This may be kept in log files for security, troubleshooting and availability.

Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs are not combined with other data and are deleted or anonymised under the hosting provider’s policy.

4. Map — loads only on click

In the “Visit” section there is a “Load the map” button. Only if you press it does your browser connect to openstreetmap.org, operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom), which then receives your IP address and the technical details of the request. Until then no connection is made.

Legal basis: your consent given by the click (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You withdraw it by closing or reloading the page; the map does not load again without a new click. The United Kingdom is covered by a European Commission adequacy decision. OSMF privacy policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy.

5. Booking through ebarber

Appointments are booked on the ebarber.gr platform, operated by BOOKING HERO I.K.E. (196 Syngrou Avenue, Kallithea 176 71, Greece, info@ebarber.gr). When you press “Book”, you leave our website and our page on ebarber opens in a new tab.

The details you enter there (name, phone, email, service, day and time) are collected by ebarber under its own privacy policy and passed to the barbershop so the appointment can take place. For the appointment details we receive, we are the controller; the legal basis is performance of the service contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). We keep them as long as needed for the appointment, any changes or cancellations, and serving you as a customer.

The website sends no marketing messages. Appointment reminders or updates sent through ebarber are governed by ebarber’s settings and policy.

6. Phone contact

If you call us, we process your number and what you tell us only to arrange your appointment or answer your question (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

7. Links to third parties

The website links to ebarber.gr, Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, YouTube (ERT) and iefimerida.gr. They open in a new tab and, once you follow them, the respective providers’ privacy policies apply. We do not embed videos, Google maps or social media buttons.

8. Cookies and browser storage

The website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser — no local storage, no session storage. That is why it shows no consent banner: under Article 4(5) of Greek Law 3471/2006, consent is required for storing or accessing information on your device that is not strictly necessary, and we do none.

If you press “Load the map”, the OpenStreetMap frame may store its own technical data to operate the map; that is governed by the OSMF policy. If we ever add analytics or other optional tools, we will ask for your consent first, with a clear choice to accept or reject.

9. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Articles 15–21 GDPR), and the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on it (Article 7(3)). To exercise them, contact us at the details in section 1.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority: 1-3 Kifisias Avenue, 115 23 Athens, Greece, www.dpa.gr, contact@dpa.gr.

10. Minors

The website is not aimed at minors and we do not knowingly collect their data. For children’s haircuts, the parent or guardian makes the booking.

11. Changes

If the way the website works changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
