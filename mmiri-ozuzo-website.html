N°01 The shop

More than a haircut.

On Kolokotroni street, between the coffee fumes and the city's noise, Mmiri Ozuzo keeps an older Athens alive: a barbershop where the chair is worn smooth by regulars, the razor is honed daily, and nobody leaves until the line at the neck is perfect.

We're not a chain, a concept or a salon. We're the shop on the corner that knows your name, your collar and exactly how your hair grows — with a 4.7 rating from 298 Google reviews to prove the regulars mean it.

N°02 The history

The first original barbershop of Athens.

Before Omonia found its rhythm and Kolokotroni filled with cafés, there was one chair here — the first. Mmiri Ozuzo opened as the first original barbershop of Athens, and every other chair in this city is, one way or another, a descendant of this counter.

I

The first chair

A single chair, a strop behind the door and one promise: nobody leaves until the line is true. Word travelled fast through the neighbourhood, and the shop became a fixture of the street.

II

The craft, passed down

Fathers brought their sons, sons brought their own. Razors honed, towels steamed, the ritual refined across generations — untouched where it matters.

III

The same standard, today

Same address, same creed. The scissors are sharper and the booking is online — but the standard we set when we cut first in Athens hasn't dropped an inch.
